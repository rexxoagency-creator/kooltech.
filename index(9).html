Garage guide

WHEN YOUR CAR FEELS WRONG.

Interactive problem cards for the everyday customer. These are guidance prompts—not a diagnosis.

01 • NO COLD AIR

Could be airflow, refrigerant, compressor, condenser or control-side trouble. Start with diagnosis instead of guessing.

02 • WEAK AIRFLOW

Check cabin filter, blower operation and blocked airflow paths before assuming the compressor is bad.

03 • BAD SMELL

Cabin filtration, moisture and evaporator-side cleanliness can all affect cabin air quality.

04 • NOISY AC

Noise can come from the compressor, blower, belt-side components or other rotating parts. Inspect first.

05 • COLD THEN WARM

Intermittent cooling needs proper pressure, temperature and electrical checks; a refill isn't automatically the answer.

06 • DUSTY CABIN

A clogged or poor cabin filter can restrict flow and degrade perceived comfort. MANN-FILTER and DENSO both publish cabin filtration products.

Quick tip

DON'T CHANGE THE PART. FIND THE CAUSE.

Car AC systems are connected systems. A compressor, expansion device, condenser, evaporator, blower and control electronics can all create similar symptoms from the driver's seat.
